Lentil

Easy Guide to Grow Lentil on 1 Acre

This guide tells you how to grow lentil on 1 acre. It’s for anyone, even beginners. Your land is ready for lentil (loamy soil, low water ~300–500 mm, some nutrients). You need 12–15 kg of seeds (use hybrid, grows in ~16 weeks, gives ~4–6 quintals).

Before Planting (Weeks -1 to 0)

Week -1: Fix the Field

  • What to Do:
  • Check soil test. If sour, add 0.5 cartload lime (~₹1000). If hard, add 0.5 cartload gypsum (~₹1000).
  • Plow field 2 times to loosen soil. Spread 0.5 cartload cow dung (~₹500).

  • Add plant food: 0.5 bag SSP (25 kg, ~₹200).

  • What to Buy:

  • Lime/gypsum: ~₹1000/half cartload.
  • Cow dung: ~₹500/half cartload.

  • SSP: ~₹200/bag.

  • Tip: Loose soil helps lentil roots grow.

Growing Lentil (Weeks 1 to 16)

Week 1: Plant Seeds

  • What to Do:

  • Buy 12–15 kg hybrid seeds (~₹800–1500). Sow seeds in rows 30 cm apart. Water well.

  • What to Buy:

  • Seeds: ~₹800–1500.

  • Tip: Plant when soil is cool.

Weeks 2–4: Help Plants Grow Roots

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 10 days (lightly).

  • Week 3: Pull weeds (~2 workers, ~₹1000).

  • What to Buy: Nothing new.

  • Tip: Remove weeds early.

Weeks 5–8: Help Plants Grow Stems

  • What to Do:

  • Water every 10 days. Week 6: Check for pod borers (holes in pods). Spray 500 ml Malathion in 200 L water (~₹400) if seen.

  • What to Buy:

  • Malathion: ~₹400/L.

  • Tip: Keep soil moist during flowering.

Weeks 9–12: Support Pod Growth

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 10 days. Check pods as they form.

  • Week 10: Spray 500 ml Malathion (~₹400) if bugs return.

  • What to Buy:

  • Malathion: ~₹400/L.

  • Tip: Pods should feel full.

Weeks 13–16: Pick Lentil

  • What to Do:
  • Stop water 1 week before picking. Pick when pods are dry (~₹2800 for labor).

  • Dry pods in sun (~₹1000 for transport).

  • What to Buy: Nothing new.

  • Tip: Expect ~4–6 quintals (~₹12,000–18,000 at ₹3000/quintal).

After Collecting Lentil

  • What to Do:
  • Clear field of plants. Store pods in a dry place.

  • Plan another crop.

  • Tip: Check stored pods for bugs.

Money You Spend and Earn

  • What You Spend:
  • Seeds: ~₹800–1500.
  • Plant food: ~₹1000–2000.
  • Pest sprays: ~₹500–1000.
  • Workers: ~₹3000–5000.

  • Total: ~₹5300–9500.

  • What You Earn: ~4–6 quintals at ₹3000/quintal = ~₹12,000–18,000. After costs, you keep ~₹2500–12,700.

  • Tip: Use cow dung to save money.

Easy Tips to Do Well

  • Water: Needs little water. Water every 10 days.
  • Check Plants: Look for bugs in pods weekly.
  • Be Safe: Wear mask when spraying.
  • Ask for Help: Ask local farmers for planting times.
  • Best Time: Plant in October–November (check local timing).
