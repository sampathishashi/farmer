Apple

# Easy Guide to Grow Apple on 1 Acre

This guide tells you how to grow apples on 1 acre. It's for anyone, even if you don't read well or never grew apples before. Your land is ready for apples (loamy soil, medium water ~800–1000 mm, cool climate). You need **100–150 apple trees** (use grafted, first harvest in ~3–4 years, gives ~5–10 tons/year). I'll tell you what to do each week, what to buy, and how to keep it simple.

## Before Planting (Weeks -2 to -1)

**Week -2: Fix the Field**

- **What to Do**:
  - Check soil test to confirm it's good for apples (pH 6–7). If too sour, add **1 cartload lime** (~₹2000). If hard, add **1 cartload gypsum** (~₹2000).
  - Plow field 2 times to make soil loose. Spread **1 cartload cow dung** (~₹1000).
  - Add plant food: **0.5 bag urea** (25 kg, ~₹265), **0.5 bag SSP** (25 kg, ~₹200), **0.5 bag MOP** (20 kg, ~₹1700).

- **What to Buy**:
  - Lime/gypsum: ~₹2000/cartload.
  - Cow dung: ~₹1000/cartload.
  - Urea: ~₹265/bag.
  - SSP: ~₹200/bag.
  - MOP: ~₹1700/bag.

- **Tip**: Loose soil helps apple roots grow strong.

**Week -1: Get Trees Ready**

- **What to Do**:
  - Buy **100–150 grafted apple trees** (~₹8000–12,000) from a trusted nursery.
  - Dig pits 60 cm deep, 60 cm wide, 4 m apart in rows. Mix soil with cow dung.

- **What to Buy**:
  - Apple trees: ~₹8000–12,000.

- **Tip**: Choose healthy trees with strong roots.

## Growing Apples (Weeks 1 to Year 4)

**Week 1: Plant Trees**

- **What to Do**:
  - Plant trees in pits. Cover roots with soil. Water well (like a good rain).
  - Keep soil moist but not flooded.

- **What to Buy**: Nothing new.
- **Tip**: Plant in the morning when it's cool.

**Weeks 2–12: Help Trees Grow Roots**

- **What to Do**:
  - Water every 7–10 days (~5 cm water).
  - **Week 6**: Spread **0.5 bag urea** (25 kg, ~₹265).
  - **Week 8**: Pull weeds by hand (~2 workers, ~₹1000).

- **What to Buy**:
  - Urea: ~₹265/bag.

- **Tip**: Keep soil weed-free for strong roots.

**Weeks 13–52 (Year 1): Help Trees Grow**

- **What to Do**:
  - Water every 7–10 days. **Week 24**: Spread **0.5 bag urea** (25 kg, ~₹265).
  - **Week 32**: Check for aphids. Spray **100 ml Imidacloprid** in 200 L water (~₹400) if seen.
  - Prune weak branches in winter (~₹1000 for labor).

- **What to Buy**:
  - Urea: ~₹265/bag.
  - Imidacloprid: ~₹400/bottle.

- **Tip**: Check for bugs weekly.

**Years 2–3: Support Growth**

- **What to Do**:
  - Water every 7–10 days. Spread **0.5 bag urea** and **0.5 bag MOP** yearly (~₹1965).
  - Check for scab (spots on leaves). Spray **500 g Mancozeb** in 200 L water (~₹300) if seen.
  - Prune yearly in winter (~₹1000/year).

- **What to Buy**:
  - Urea: ~₹265/bag.
  - MOP: ~₹1700/bag.
  - Mancozeb: ~₹300/kg.

- **Tip**: Pruning helps trees grow stronger.

**Year 4: Pick Apples**

- **What to Do**:
  - Water until 1 month before picking. Pick apples when firm and colored (~₹5000 for labor).
  - Store in a cool place (~₹1000 for transport).

- **What to Buy**: Nothing new.
- **Tip**: Expect ~5–10 tons/year (~₹50,000–1,00,000 at ₹10,000/ton).

## After Collecting Apples

- **What to Do**:
  - Prune trees after harvest. Rest the soil or add cow dung.
  - Store apples in a cool, dry place.

- **Tip**: Check stored apples for rot monthly.

## Money You Spend and Earn

- **What You Spend**:
  - Trees: ~₹8000–12,000.
  - Plant food (fertilizers): ~₹3000–6000 (over 4 years).
  - Pest sprays: ~₹500–1000.
  - Workers (planting, weeding, pruning, picking): ~₹5000–8000.
  - **Total**: ~₹16,500–27,000 (over 4 years).

- **What You Earn**: ~5–10 tons/year at ₹10,000/ton = ~₹50,000–1,00,000/year. After costs, you keep ~₹23,000–83,500 (first harvest year).

- **Tip**: Save money by using cow dung.

## Easy Tips to Do Well

- **Water**: Keep soil moist, water every 7–10 days.
- **Check Trees**: Walk field weekly to spot bugs or spots.
- **Be Safe**: Wear a mask and gloves when spraying. Ask a farm helper about safe use.
- **Ask for Help**: Talk to local farmers or a farm office for planting times or spray advice.
- **Best Time**: Plant in January–February (check local timing).

  - Add plant food: **1 bag urea** (50 kg, ~₹265), **1.5 bags SSP** (75 kg, ~₹200/bag), **0.5 bag MOP** (20 kg, ~₹1700). If soil lacks zinc, add **25 kg zinc sulfate** (~₹50/kg).

**Week -1: Get Seeds and Seed Bed Ready**

- **What to Do**:
  - Buy **8 kg PR 126 seeds** (~₹500–1000) from a trusted shop.
  - Soak seeds in water with **20 g Carbendazim** (~₹300/pack) and **1 g Streptocycline** (~₹100/pack) overnight to prevent diseases. Dry in shade.
  - Make a small seed bed (40 m², like a small room). Mix soil with cow dung, spread 4 cm thick, and sow seeds evenly.
  - Water seed bed daily to keep it wet like a sponge. Add a handful of urea, SSP, and MOP.

- **What to Buy**:
  - Carbendazim: ~₹300/pack.
  - Streptocycline: ~₹100/pack.

- **Tip**: Keep seed bed wet but not flooded. Seeds grow into small plants in 15–21 days.

- **What to Do**:
  - Check seed bed. Plants should be 15–21 days old, ~25 cm tall (like a ruler).

  - Water daily to maintain muddy field.

- **What to Buy**: Nothing new.
- **Tip**: Plant in the morning. Handle plants gently to avoid breaking.

- **What to Do**:

  - **Week 5**: Check for blast disease (brown spots on leaves). Spray **500 g Zineb** in 200 L water (~₹300/kg) if seen.
