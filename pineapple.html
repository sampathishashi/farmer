Pineapple

Easy Guide to Grow Pineapple on 1 Acre

This guide tells you how to grow pineapples on 1 acre. It’s for anyone, even if you don’t read well or never grew pineapples before. Your land is ready for pineapples (sandy loamy soil, high water ~1000–1500 mm, enough nutrients). You need 10,000–15,000 pineapple suckers (use hybrid, grows in ~18 months, gives ~10–15 tons). I’ll tell you what to do each week, what to buy, and how to keep it simple.

Before Planting (Weeks -2 to -1)

Week -2: Fix the Field

  • What to Do:
  • Check soil test to confirm it’s good for pineapples (pH 4.5–5.5). If not acidic, add 1 cartload lime (~₹2000).
  • Plow field 2 times to make soil loose. Spread 1 cartload cow dung (~₹1000).

  • Add plant food: 0.5 bag urea (25 kg, ~₹265), 0.5 bag SSP (25 kg, ~₹200), 0.5 bag MOP (20 kg, ~₹1700).

  • What to Buy:

  • Lime: ~₹2000/cartload.
  • Cow dung: ~₹1000/cartload.
  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.
  • SSP: ~₹200/bag.

  • MOP: ~₹1700/bag.

  • Tip: Loose, acidic soil helps pineapple roots grow strong.

Week -1: Get Suckers Ready

  • What to Do:
  • Buy 10,000–15,000 pineapple suckers (~₹10,000–15,000) from a trusted supplier.

  • Prepare beds 30 cm high, 90 cm wide, 30 cm apart. Mix soil with cow dung.

  • What to Buy:

  • Pineapple suckers: ~₹10,000–15,000.

  • Tip: Choose healthy suckers with green leaves.

Growing Pineapples (Weeks 1 to 78)

Week 1: Plant Suckers

  • What to Do:
  • Plant suckers 30 cm apart in beds. Cover roots with soil. Water well (like a good rain).

  • Keep soil moist but not flooded.

  • What to Buy: Nothing new.

  • Tip: Plant in the morning when it’s cool.

Weeks 2–12: Help Plants Grow Roots

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 5–7 days (~5 cm water).
  • Week 6: Spread 0.5 bag urea (25 kg, ~₹265).

  • Week 8: Pull weeds by hand (~2 workers, ~₹1000).

  • What to Buy:

  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.

  • Tip: Keep soil weed-free for strong roots.

Weeks 13–36: Help Plants Grow

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 5–7 days. Week 24: Spread 0.5 bag urea (25 kg, ~₹265).

  • Week 32: Check for mealybugs. Spray 100 ml Imidacloprid in 200 L water (~₹400) if seen.

  • What to Buy:

  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.

  • Imidacloprid: ~₹400/bottle.

  • Tip: Check for bugs weekly.

Weeks 37–78: Pick Pineapples

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 5–7 days. Week 48: Spread 0.5 bag MOP (20 kg, ~₹1700).
  • Week 60: Check for fruit rot. Spray 500 g Mancozeb in 200 L water (~₹300) if seen.

  • Pick pineapples when yellow (~₹5000 for labor). Store in a cool place (~₹1000 for transport).

  • What to Buy:

  • MOP: ~₹1700/bag.

  • Mancozeb: ~₹300/kg.

  • Tip: Expect ~10–15 tons (~₹1,00,000–1,50,000 at ₹10,000/ton).

After Collecting Pineapples

  • What to Do:
  • Keep suckers for the next crop. Clear old plants.

  • Store pineapples in a cool, dry place.

  • Tip: Check stored pineapples for rot weekly.

Money You Spend and Earn

  • What You Spend:
  • Suckers: ~₹10,000–15,000.
  • Plant food (fertilizers): ~₹3000–5000.
  • Pest sprays: ~₹500–1000.
  • Workers (planting, weeding, picking): ~₹5000–8000.

  • Total: ~₹18,500–29,000.

  • What You Earn: ~10–15 tons at ₹10,000/ton = ~₹1,00,000–1,50,000. After costs, you keep ~₹71,000–1,31,500.

  • Tip: Save money by using cow dung.

Easy Tips to Do Well

  • Water: Keep soil moist, water every 5–7 days.
  • Check Plants: Walk field weekly to spot bugs or rot.
  • Be Safe: Wear a mask and gloves when spraying. Ask a farm helper about safe use.
  • Ask for Help: Talk to local farmers or a farm office for planting times or spray advice.
