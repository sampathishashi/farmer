Maize

Easy Guide to Grow Maize on 1 Acre

This guide tells you how to grow maize on 1 acre. It’s for anyone, even if you don’t read well. Your land is ready for maize (loamy soil, medium water ~500–1000 mm, enough nutrients). You need 8–10 kg of maize seeds (use hybrid, grows in ~14 weeks, gives ~20–25 quintals).

Before Planting (Weeks -1 to 0)

Week -1: Fix the Field

  • What to Do:
  • Check soil test. If sour, add 1 cartload lime (~₹2000). If hard, add 1 cartload gypsum (~₹2000).
  • Plow field 2 times to loosen soil. Spread 1 cartload cow dung (~₹1000).

  • Add plant food: 1 bag urea (50 kg, ~₹265), 1.5 bags SSP (75 kg, ~₹200/bag), 0.5 bag MOP (20 kg, ~₹1700).

  • What to Buy:

  • Lime/gypsum: ~₹2000/cartload.
  • Cow dung: ~₹1000/cartload.
  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.
  • SSP: ~₹200/bag.

  • MOP: ~₹1700/bag.

  • Tip: Loose soil helps maize roots grow strong.

Growing Maize (Weeks 1 to 14)

Week 1: Plant Seeds

  • What to Do:

  • Buy 8–10 kg hybrid seeds (~₹1000–2000). Sow seeds in rows 60 cm apart, 25 cm between plants. Water well.

  • What to Buy:

  • Seeds: ~₹1000–2000.

  • Tip: Plant when soil is warm, not too wet.

Weeks 2–4: Help Plants Grow Roots

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 7 days (like a good rain).
  • Week 3: Spread 0.5 bag urea (25 kg, ~₹265).

  • Week 4: Pull weeds (~2 workers, ~₹1000) or use 0.5 L Pendimethalin (~₹500).

  • What to Buy:

  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.

  • Pendimethalin: ~₹500/L.

  • Tip: Check for weeds early to save plants.

Weeks 5–8: Help Plants Grow Stems and Leaves

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 7 days. Week 6: Spread 0.5 bag urea (25 kg, ~₹265).

  • Week 7: Check for stem borers (holes in stems). Spray 500 ml Malathion in 200 L water (~₹400) if seen.

  • What to Buy:

  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.

  • Malathion: ~₹400/L.

  • Tip: Keep soil moist for strong cobs.

Weeks 9–12: Support Cob Growth

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 7 days. Check cobs as they form.

  • Week 10: Spray 500 ml Malathion in 200 L water (~₹400) if bugs return.

  • What to Buy:

  • Malathion: ~₹400/L.

  • Tip: Cobs should feel full and firm.

Weeks 13–14: Pick Maize

  • What to Do:
  • Stop water 1 week before picking. Pick cobs when kernels are hard (~₹2800 for labor).

  • Dry cobs in sun (~₹1000 for transport).

  • What to Buy: Nothing new.

  • Tip: Expect ~20–25 quintals (~₹40,000–50,000 at ₹2000/quintal).

After Collecting Maize

  • What to Do:
  • Clear field of stalks. Store cobs in a dry place.

  • Plan another crop to keep soil good.

  • Tip: Check stored maize for bugs.

Money You Spend and Earn

  • What You Spend:
  • Seeds: ~₹1000–2000.
  • Plant food: ~₹2000–4000.
  • Pest sprays: ~₹500–1000.
  • Workers: ~₹3000–5000.

  • Total: ~₹6500–12,000.

  • What You Earn: ~20–25 quintals at ₹2000/quintal = ~₹40,000–50,000. After costs, you keep ~₹28,000–43,500.

  • Tip: Use cow dung to save on fertilizers.

Easy Tips to Do Well

  • Water: Water weekly, not too much like rice.
  • Check Plants: Look for bugs or yellow leaves weekly.
  • Be Safe: Wear mask when spraying.
  • Ask for Help: Ask local farmers for planting times.
  • Best Time: Plant in June–July (check local timing).
