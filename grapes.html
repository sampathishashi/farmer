Grapes

Easy Guide to Grow Grapes on 1 Acre

This guide tells you how to grow grapes on 1 acre. It’s for anyone, even if you don’t read well or never grew grapes before. Your land is ready for grapes (loamy soil, medium water ~600–800 mm, enough nutrients). You need 400–500 grape vines (use grafted, first harvest in ~2–3 years, gives ~5–8 tons/year). I’ll tell you what to do each week, what to buy, and how to keep it simple.

Before Planting (Weeks -2 to -1)

Week -2: Fix the Field

  • What to Do:
  • Check soil test to confirm it’s good for grapes (pH 6–7). If too sour, add 1 cartload lime (~₹2000). If hard, add 1 cartload gypsum (~₹2000).
  • Plow field 2 times to make soil loose. Spread 1 cartload cow dung (~₹1000).

  • Add plant food: 0.5 bag urea (25 kg, ~₹265), 0.5 bag SSP (25 kg, ~₹200), 0.5 bag MOP (20 kg, ~₹1700).

  • What to Buy:

  • Lime/gypsum: ~₹2000/cartload.
  • Cow dung: ~₹1000/cartload.
  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.
  • SSP: ~₹200/bag.

  • MOP: ~₹1700/bag.

  • Tip: Loose soil helps grape roots grow strong.

Week -1: Get Vines Ready

  • What to Do:
  • Buy 400–500 grafted grape vines (~₹8000–10,000) from a trusted nursery.
  • Dig pits 60 cm deep, 60 cm wide, 2 m apart in rows. Set up trellises (wire supports) (~₹2000).

  • Mix soil with cow dung.

  • What to Buy:

  • Grape vines: ~₹8000–10,000.

  • Trellis materials: ~₹2000.

  • Tip: Choose healthy vines with strong roots.

Growing Grapes (Weeks 1 to Year 3)

Week 1: Plant Vines

  • What to Do:
  • Plant vines in pits. Cover roots with soil. Water well (like a good rain).

  • Tie vines to trellises. Keep soil moist but not flooded.

  • What to Buy: Nothing new.

  • Tip: Plant in the morning when it’s cool.

Weeks 2–12: Help Vines Grow Roots

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 7–10 days (~5 cm water).
  • Week 6: Spread 0.5 bag urea (25 kg, ~₹265).

  • Week 8: Pull weeds by hand (~2 workers, ~₹1000).

  • What to Buy:

  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.

  • Tip: Keep soil weed-free for strong roots.

Weeks 13–52 (Year 1): Help Vines Grow

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 7–10 days. Week 24: Spread 0.5 bag urea (25 kg, ~₹265).
  • Week 32: Check for powdery mildew (white spots). Spray 500 g Mancozeb in 200 L water (~₹300) if seen.

  • Prune weak branches in winter (~₹1000 for labor).

  • What to Buy:

  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.

  • Mancozeb: ~₹300/kg.

  • Tip: Pruning helps vines grow stronger.

Year 2: Support Growth

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 7–10 days. Spread 0.5 bag urea and 0.5 bag MOP (~₹1965).
  • Check for fruit flies. Spray 500 ml Malathion in 200 L water (~₹400) if seen.

  • Prune in winter (~₹1000).

  • What to Buy:

  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.
  • MOP: ~₹1700/bag.

  • Malathion: ~₹400/L.

  • Tip: Train vines on trellises for better fruit.

Year 3: Pick Grapes

  • What to Do:
  • Water until 1 month before picking. Pick grapes when full and colored (~₹5000 for labor).

  • Store in a cool place (~₹1000 for transport).

  • What to Buy: Nothing new.

  • Tip: Expect ~5–8 tons/year (~₹50,000–80,000 at ₹10,000/ton).

After Collecting Grapes

  • What to Do:
  • Prune vines after harvest. Rest the soil or add cow dung.

  • Store grapes in a cool, dry place.

  • Tip: Check stored grapes for rot weekly.

Money You Spend and Earn

  • What You Spend:
  • Vines: ~₹8000–10,000.
  • Trellis: ~₹2000.
  • Plant food (fertilizers): ~₹3000–6000 (over 3 years).
  • Pest sprays: ~₹500–1000.
  • Workers (planting, weeding, pruning, picking): ~₹5000–8000.

  • Total: ~₹18,500–27,000 (over 3 years).

  • What You Earn: ~5–8 tons/year at ₹10,000/ton = ~₹50,000–80,000/year. After costs, you keep ~₹23,000–61,500 (first harvest year).

  • Tip: Save money by using cow dung.

Easy Tips to Do Well

  • Water: Keep soil moist, water every 7–10 days.
  • Check Vines: Walk field weekly to spot bugs or spots.
  • Be Safe: Wear a mask and gloves when spraying. Ask a farm helper about safe use.
  • Ask for Help: Talk to local farmers or a farm office for planting times or spray advice.
  • Best Time: Plant in January–February (check local timing).
