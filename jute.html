Jute

Easy Guide to Grow Jute on 1 Acre

This guide tells you how to grow jute on 1 acre. It’s for anyone, even if you don’t read well or never grew jute before. Your land is ready for jute (muddy or loamy soil, high water ~1500–2000 mm, enough nutrients). You need 3–4 kg of jute seeds (use hybrid, grows in ~16 weeks, gives ~10–12 quintals). I’ll tell you what to do each week, what to buy, and how to keep it simple.

Before Planting (Weeks -1 to 0)

Week -1: Fix the Field

  • What to Do:
  • Check soil test to confirm it’s good for jute. If soil is too sour, add 1 cartload lime (~₹2000). If hard, add 1 cartload gypsum (~₹2000).
  • Plow field 2–3 times with ~5 cm water (like a muddy puddle). Spread 1 cartload cow dung (~₹1000).

  • Add plant food: 0.5 bag urea (25 kg, ~₹265), 0.5 bag SSP (25 kg, ~₹200), 0.5 bag MOP (20 kg, ~₹1700).

  • What to Buy:

  • Lime/gypsum: ~₹2000/cartload.
  • Cow dung: ~₹1000/cartload.
  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.
  • SSP: ~₹200/bag.

  • MOP: ~₹1700/bag.

  • Tip: Wet soil helps jute grow strong.

Growing Jute (Weeks 1 to 16)

Week 1: Plant Seeds

  • What to Do:

  • Buy 3–4 kg hybrid jute seeds (~₹500–1000). Sow seeds in rows 30 cm apart. Water well (~5 cm water).

  • What to Buy:

  • Seeds: ~₹500–1000.

  • Tip: Plant in the morning when it’s cool.

Weeks 2–4: Help Plants Grow Roots

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 7 days (~5–10 cm water).

  • Week 3: Pull weeds by hand (~2 workers, ~₹1000) or use 0.5 L Pendimethalin (~₹500).

  • What to Buy:

  • Pendimethalin: ~₹500/L.

  • Tip: Keep field wet to reduce weeds.

Weeks 5–8: Help Plants Grow Stems

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 7 days (~10 cm water).
  • Week 6: Spread 0.5 bag urea (25 kg, ~₹265).

  • Week 7: Check for stem borers (holes in stems). Spray 500 ml Malathion in 200 L water (~₹400) if seen.

  • What to Buy:

  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.

  • Malathion: ~₹400/L.

  • Tip: Check for bugs weekly.

Weeks 9–12: Support Growth

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 7 days (~10 cm water).

  • Week 10: Spray 500 g Mancozeb in 200 L water (~₹300) if leaf spots appear.

  • What to Buy:

  • Mancozeb: ~₹300/kg.

  • Tip: Keep soil wet for tall stems.

Weeks 13–16: Cut Jute

  • What to Do:
  • Stop water 1 week before cutting. Cut when stems are tall and yellow (~₹2800 for labor).

  • Soak stems in water for 10–15 days to rett (soften fibers). Dry fibers (~₹1000 for transport).

  • What to Buy: Nothing new.

  • Tip: Expect ~10–12 quintals (~₹30,000–36,000 at ₹3000/quintal).

After Collecting Jute

  • What to Do:
  • Clear field of leftover plants. Store fibers in a dry place.

  • Plan another crop to keep soil healthy.

  • Tip: Check stored fibers for moisture or bugs.

Money You Spend and Earn

  • What You Spend:
  • Seeds: ~₹500–1000.
  • Plant food (fertilizers): ~₹2000–4000.
  • Pest sprays: ~₹500–1000.
  • Workers (planting, weeding, cutting): ~₹3000–5000.

  • Total: ~₹6000–11,000.

  • What You Earn: ~10–12 quintals at ₹3000/quintal = ~₹30,000–36,000. After costs, you keep ~₹19,000–30,000.

  • Tip: Save money by using cow dung instead of some fertilizers.

Easy Tips to Do Well

  • Water: Keep field wet, like rice. Water every 7 days.
  • Check Plants: Walk field weekly to spot bugs or yellow leaves.
  • Be Safe: Wear a mask and gloves when spraying. Ask a farm helper about safe use.
  • Ask for Help: Talk to local farmers or a farm office for planting times or spray advice.
  • Best Time: Plant in March–April (check local timing).
