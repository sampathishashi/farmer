Sunflower

Easy Guide to Grow Sunflower on 1 Acre

This guide tells you how to grow sunflower on 1 acre. It’s for anyone, even if you don’t read well or never grew sunflower before. Your land is ready for sunflower (loamy soil, medium water ~500–700 mm, some nutrients). You need 2–3 kg of sunflower seeds (use hybrid, grows in ~14 weeks, gives ~5–7 quintals).

Before Planting (Weeks -1 to 0)

Week -1: Fix the Field

  • What to Do:
  • Check soil test to confirm it’s good for sunflower. If soil is too sour, add 0.5 cartload lime (~₹1000). If hard, add 0.5 cartload gypsum (~₹1000).
  • Plow field 2 times to make soil loose. Spread 1 cartload cow dung (~₹1000).

  • Add plant food: 0.5 bag urea (25 kg, ~₹265), 0.5 bag SSP (25 kg, ~₹200), 0.5 bag MOP (20 kg, ~₹1700).

  • What to Buy:

  • Lime/gypsum: ~₹1000/half cartload.
  • Cow dung: ~₹1000/cartload.
  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.
  • SSP: ~₹200/bag.

  • MOP: ~₹1700/bag.

  • Tip: Loose soil helps sunflower roots grow strong.

Growing Sunflower (Weeks 1 to 14)

Week 1: Plant Seeds

  • What to Do:

  • Buy 2–3 kg hybrid sunflower seeds (~₹500–1000). Sow seeds in rows 60 cm apart, 30 cm between plants. Water well after planting.

  • What to Buy:

  • Seeds: ~₹500–1000.

  • Tip: Plant in the evening when it’s cool.

Weeks 2–4: Help Plants Grow Roots

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 7–10 days (like a good rain).

  • Week 3: Pull weeds by hand (~2 workers, ~₹1000) or use 0.5 L Pendimethalin (~₹500).

  • What to Buy:

  • Pendimethalin: ~₹500/L.

  • Tip: Check for weeds early to save plants.

Weeks 5–8: Help Plants Grow Stems and Flowers

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 7–10 days. Week 6: Spread 0.5 bag urea (25 kg, ~₹265) to help plants grow.

  • Week 7: Check for leaf-eating bugs or head borers. Spray 500 ml Malathion in 200 L water (~₹400) if seen.

  • What to Buy:

  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.

  • Malathion: ~₹400/L.

  • Tip: Keep soil moist during flower formation.

Weeks 9–12: Support Seed Growth

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 7–10 days. Check flower heads as seeds form.

  • Week 10: Spray 500 ml Malathion (~₹400) if bugs return.

  • What to Buy:

  • Malathion: ~₹400/L.

  • Tip: Seeds should feel full and firm.

Weeks 13–14: Pick Sunflower

  • What to Do:
  • Stop water 1 week before picking. Cut heads when seeds are hard (~₹2800 for labor).

  • Dry seeds in the sun (~₹1000 for transport to market).

  • What to Buy: Nothing new.

  • Tip: Expect ~5–7 quintals (~₹20,000–28,000 at ₹4000/quintal).

After Collecting Sunflower

  • What to Do:
  • Clear field of leftover plants. Store seeds in a dry place safe from bugs.

  • Plan another crop to keep soil healthy.

  • Tip: Check stored seeds for bugs monthly.

Money You Spend and Earn

  • What You Spend:
  • Seeds: ~₹500–1000.
  • Plant food (fertilizers): ~₹2000–4000.
  • Pest sprays: ~₹500–1000.
  • Workers (planting, weeding, picking): ~₹3000–5000.

  • Total: ~₹6000–11,000.

  • What You Earn: ~5–7 quintals at ₹4000/quintal = ~₹20,000–28,000. After costs, you keep ~₹9000–22,000.

  • Tip: Save money by using cow dung instead of some fertilizers.

Easy Tips to Do Well

  • Water: Sunflower needs medium water. Water every 7–10 days.
  • Check Plants: Walk field weekly to spot bugs or yellow leaves.
  • Be Safe: Wear a mask and gloves when spraying. Ask a farm helper about safe use.
  • Ask for Help: Talk to local farmers or a farm office for planting times or spray advice.
  • Best Time: Plant in June–July (check local timing).
