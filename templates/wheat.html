Wheat

Easy Guide to Grow Wheat on 1 Acre

This guide tells you how to grow wheat on 1 acre. It’s for anyone, even if you don’t read well or never grew wheat before. Your land is ready for wheat (muddy or loamy soil, medium water ~500–800 mm, enough nutrients). You need 40 kg of wheat seeds (use HD 2967, grows in ~20 weeks, gives ~18–20 quintals). I’ll tell you what to do each week, what to buy, and how to keep it simple.

Before Planting (Weeks -1 to 0)

Week -1: Fix the Field

  • What to Do:
  • Check soil test to confirm it’s good for wheat. If soil is too sour, add 1 cartload lime (~₹2000). If hard, add 1 cartload gypsum (~₹2000).
  • Plow field 2–3 times to make soil loose. Spread 1 cartload cow dung (~₹1000).

  • Add plant food: 1 bag urea (50 kg, ~₹265), 1 bag SSP (50 kg, ~₹200), 0.5 bag MOP (20 kg, ~₹1700).

  • What to Buy:

  • Lime/gypsum: ~₹2000/cartload.
  • Cow dung: ~₹1000/cartload.
  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.
  • SSP: ~₹200/bag.

  • MOP: ~₹1700/bag.

  • Tip: Plow when soil is slightly wet for best results.

Growing Wheat (Weeks 1 to 20)

Week 1: Plant Seeds

  • What to Do:

  • Buy 40 kg HD 2967 seeds (~₹1000–2000). Sow seeds in rows 20 cm apart using a seed drill or by hand. Water lightly after planting.

  • What to Buy:

  • Seeds: ~₹1000–2000.

  • Tip: Plant in the evening to keep seeds safe from heat.

Weeks 2–4: Help Plants Grow Roots

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 10 days (like a light shower).
  • Week 3: Spread 0.5 bag urea (25 kg, ~₹265) to help plants grow.

  • Week 4: Pull weeds by hand (~2 workers, ~₹1000) or ask farm helper about safe weed-killer like 0.5 L Pendimethalin (~₹500).

  • What to Buy:

  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.

  • Pendimethalin: ~₹500/L.

  • Tip: Check for weeds early to save plants.

Weeks 5–8: Help Plants Grow Stems

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 10 days. Keep soil moist but not flooded.

  • Week 6: Check for rust disease (orange spots on leaves). Spray 500 g Mancozeb in 200 L water (~₹300) if seen.

  • What to Buy:

  • Mancozeb: ~₹300/kg.

  • Tip: Walk field to spot yellow leaves or spots.

Weeks 9–12: Support Flowering

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 10 days. Week 10: Spread 0.5 bag urea (25 kg, ~₹265).

  • Check for aphids (small bugs on stems). Spray 100 ml Imidacloprid in 200 L water (~₹400) if seen.

  • What to Buy:

  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.

  • Imidacloprid: ~₹400/bottle.

  • Tip: Keep water steady during flowering.

Weeks 13–16: Help Grains Grow

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 10 days. Check grains as they form.

  • Week 14: Spray 500 g Mancozeb in 200 L water (~₹300) if rust spots return.

  • What to Buy:

  • Mancozeb: ~₹300/kg.

  • Tip: Grains should feel hard when touched.

Weeks 17–20: Cut and Collect Wheat

  • What to Do:
  • Week 17: Stop water 2 weeks before cutting.

  • Week 20: Cut when grains are hard and straw is golden (~₹2800 for machine). Thresh and dry grains in sun (~₹1000 for transport).

  • What to Buy: Nothing new.

  • Tip: Expect ~18–20 quintals (~₹30,000–40,000 at ₹2000/quintal).

After Collecting Wheat

  • What to Do:
  • Clear field of stalks. Store grains in a dry place.

  • Plan a summer crop to keep soil healthy.

  • Tip: Check stored grains for bugs monthly.

Money You Spend and Earn

  • What You Spend:
  • Seeds: ~₹1000–2000.
  • Plant food: ~₹2000–4000.
  • Pest sprays: ~₹500–1000.
  • Workers: ~₹3000–5000.

  • Total: ~₹6500–12,000.

  • What You Earn: ~18–20 quintals at ₹2000/quintal = ~₹30,000–40,000. After costs, you keep ~₹18,000–33,500.

  • Tip: Use cow dung to lower fertilizer costs.

Easy Tips to Do Well

  • Water: Water lightly every 10 days, not like rice.
  • Check Plants: Look for bugs or yellow leaves weekly.
  • Be Safe: Wear mask when spraying.
  • Ask for Help: Consult local farmers for planting times.
  • Best Time: Plant in November (check local timing).
