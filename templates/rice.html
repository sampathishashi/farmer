Rice

Easy Guide to Grow Rice on 1 Acre

This guide tells you how to grow rice on 1 acre of land. It’s for anyone, even if you don’t read well or never grew rice before. Your land is ready for rice (muddy or loamy soil, good water ~1000–1500 mm, enough nutrients). You need 8 kg of rice seeds (use PR 126, grows in ~17 weeks, gives ~22–30 quintals). I’ll tell you what to do each week, what to buy (like fertilizers or pest sprays), and how to keep it simple.

Before Planting (Weeks -2 to -1)

Week -2: Fix the Field

  • What to Do:
  • Check soil test to confirm it’s good for rice. If soil is too sour (acidic), spread 2 cartloads lime (~₹2000/cartload). If soil is hard, add 1 cartload gypsum (~₹2000).
  • Plow field 2–3 times with ~5 cm water (like a muddy puddle). Make it flat using a tractor or tool.
  • Spread 2 cartloads cow dung (~₹1000/cartload) or plant 20 kg dhaincha seeds (bury after 6 weeks to enrich soil).

  • Add plant food: 1 bag urea (50 kg, ~₹265), 1.5 bags SSP (75 kg, ~₹200/bag), 0.5 bag MOP (20 kg, ~₹1700). If soil lacks zinc, add 25 kg zinc sulfate (~₹50/kg).

  • What to Buy:

  • Lime/gypsum: ~₹2000/cartload.
  • Cow dung: ~₹1000/cartload.
  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.
  • SSP: ~₹200/bag.
  • MOP: ~₹1700/bag.
  • Zinc sulfate: ~₹1250 (25 kg).

  • Dhaincha seeds: ~₹1000 (optional).

  • Tip: Ask a farm helper about soil. Spread cow dung evenly like a blanket.

Week -1: Get Seeds and Seed Bed Ready

  • What to Do:
  • Buy 8 kg PR 126 seeds (~₹500–1000) from a trusted shop.
  • Soak seeds in water with 20 g Carbendazim (~₹300/pack) and 1 g Streptocycline (~₹100/pack) overnight to prevent diseases. Dry in shade.
  • Make a small seed bed (40 m², like a small room). Mix soil with cow dung, spread 4 cm thick, and sow seeds evenly.

  • Water seed bed daily to keep it wet like a sponge. Add a handful of urea, SSP, and MOP.

  • What to Buy:

  • Carbendazim: ~₹300/pack.

  • Streptocycline: ~₹100/pack.

  • Tip: Keep seed bed wet but not flooded. Seeds grow into small plants in 15–21 days.

Growing Rice (Weeks 1 to 17)

Week 1: Plant Small Plants in the Field

  • What to Do:
  • Check seed bed. Plants should be 15–21 days old, ~25 cm tall (like a ruler).
  • Gently pull plants and transplant to main field, 30 cm apart in rows, 25 cm between plants. Keep field wet with ~5 cm water.

  • Water daily to maintain muddy field.

  • What to Buy: Nothing new.

  • Tip: Plant in the morning. Handle plants gently to avoid breaking.

Weeks 2–3: Help Plants Grow Roots and Stems

  • What to Do:
  • Week 2, Day 1: Spread 0.5 bag urea (25 kg, ~₹265) to help plants grow strong.
  • Keep 5–10 cm water in field to reduce weeds.
  • Check for weeds. Pull by hand (~2 workers, ~₹1000) or ask farm helper about safe weed-killer.

  • Look for bugs like stem borers (tiny holes in stems). If seen, spray 0.5 L Chlorpyrifos in 200 L water (~₹400/L).

  • What to Buy:

  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.

  • Chlorpyrifos: ~₹400/L.

  • Tip: Walk field to spot bugs or weeds. Wet field helps control weeds.

Weeks 4–6: Help Plants Grow More Stems

  • What to Do:
  • Keep 5–15 cm water in field.
  • Week 4: If weeds remain, use 1 L Pendimethalin in 200 L water (~₹500). Confirm with farm helper.
  • Week 5: Check for blast disease (brown spots on leaves). Spray 500 g Zineb in 200 L water (~₹300/kg) if seen.

  • Week 6: If plants look yellow, add 10 kg urea (~₹265).

  • What to Buy:

  • Pendimethalin: ~₹500/L.
  • Zineb: ~₹300/kg.

  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.

  • Tip: Check plants often. Yellow leaves mean more plant food needed.

Weeks 7–9: Get Plants Ready for Flowers

  • What to Do:
  • Week 7, Day 1: Spread 0.5 bag urea (25 kg, ~₹265) to support flowering.
  • Keep 10–15 cm water in field.
  • Check for bacterial blight (wet leaf spots). If seen, spray 1 g Streptocycline in 10 L water (~₹100).

  • Weeks 8–9: Look for sheath blight (gray stem patches). Spray 200 ml Tilt in 200 L water (~₹600) if seen.

  • What to Buy:

  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.
  • Streptocycline: ~₹100/pack.

  • Tilt: ~₹600/bottle.

  • Tip: Wear mask when spraying. Keep field wet for healthy plants.

Weeks 10–12: Help Flowers and Grains Start

  • What to Do:
  • Keep 15–25 cm water to support flowers and grains.
  • Week 10, Day 1: Spray 200 ml Tilt in 200 L water (~₹600) to prevent diseases.
  • Week 11: If leaves are yellow, spray 2 kg urea in 200 L water (~₹265).

  • Week 12: Check for brown plant hoppers (small bugs at plant base). Spray 100 ml Imidacloprid in 200 L water (~₹400) if many are seen.

  • What to Buy:

  • Tilt: ~₹600/bottle.
  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.

  • Imidacloprid: ~₹400/bottle.

  • Tip: Don’t let field dry during flowering. Check for bugs often.

Weeks 13–15: Help Grains Grow Big

  • What to Do:
  • Keep 10–15 cm water, then reduce to 5 cm as grains harden.
  • Week 13: Check for false smut (yellowish grains) or neck blast (black grain heads). Spray 200 g Carbendazim in 200 L water (~₹300) if seen.

  • Weeks 14–15: Maintain water to fill grains.

  • What to Buy:

  • Carbendazim: ~₹300/pack.

  • Tip: Grains should feel firm and look golden.

Weeks 16–17: Cut and Collect Rice

  • What to Do:
  • Week 16: Check grains. When straw is yellow and grains are hard, prepare to cut.
  • Week 17, Day 1–3: Stop water 7–10 days before cutting. Cut with sickle or machine (~₹2800). Thresh grains immediately.

  • Week 17, Day 4–7: Dry grains in sun until not sticky (~₹1000 for transport to market).

  • What to Buy: Nothing new.

  • Tip: Expect ~22–30 quintals (~₹44,000–60,000 at ₹2000/quintal).

After Collecting Rice

  • What to Do:
  • Clear field of rice stalks.
  • Store grains in a dry, bug-free place.

  • Ask farm helper about growing a second crop (ratoon) by re-watering.

  • Tip: Rest field or plan another crop to keep soil healthy.

Money You Spend and Earn

  • What You Spend:
  • Seeds: ~₹500–1000.
  • Plant food (fertilizers): ~₹3000–5000.
  • Pest sprays: ~₹1000–2000.
  • Workers (planting, weeding, cutting): ~₹5000–8000.

  • Total: ~₹9500–16,000.

  • What You Earn: ~22–30 quintals at ₹2000/quintal = ~₹44,000–60,000. After costs, you keep ~₹28,000–50,000.

  • Tip: Save money by using cow dung instead of some fertilizers.

Easy Tips to Do Well

  • Water: Keep field wet or flooded as listed. Use canal or pump if rain stops.
  • Check Plants: Walk field 2–3 times weekly to spot bugs, diseases, or yellow leaves.
  • Be Safe: Wear mask and gloves when spraying. Ask farm helper for safe use.
  • Ask for Help: Talk to local farmers or farm office for planting times or spray advice.
  • Best Time: Plant in late May–early June (check local timing).
