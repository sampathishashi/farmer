Sugarcane

Easy Guide to Grow Sugarcane on 1 Acre

This guide tells you how to grow sugarcane on 1 acre. It’s for anyone, even if you don’t read well or never grew sugarcane before. Your land is ready for sugarcane (loamy or muddy soil, high water ~1500–2000 mm, enough nutrients). You need 2–3 tons of sugarcane setts (use hybrid, grows in ~48 weeks, gives ~30–40 tons). I’ll tell you what to do each week, what to buy, and how to keep it simple.

Before Planting (Weeks -2 to -1)

Week -2: Fix the Field

  • What to Do:
  • Check soil test to confirm it’s good for sugarcane. If soil is too sour, add 2 cartloads lime (~₹2000/cartload). If hard, add 1 cartload gypsum (~₹2000).
  • Plow field 3 times to make soil loose. Spread 2 cartloads cow dung (~₹1000/cartload).

  • Add plant food: 1 bag urea (50 kg, ~₹265), 2 bags SSP (100 kg, ~₹200/bag), 0.5 bag MOP (20 kg, ~₹1700).

  • What to Buy:

  • Lime/gypsum: ~₹2000/cartload.
  • Cow dung: ~₹1000/cartload.
  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.
  • SSP: ~₹200/bag.

  • MOP: ~₹1700/bag.

  • Tip: Loose soil helps sugarcane roots grow strong. Spread cow dung like a blanket.

Week -1: Get Setts Ready

  • What to Do:
  • Buy 2–3 tons sugarcane setts (~₹4000–6000) from a trusted supplier. Choose healthy stems with 2–3 buds each.
  • Cut setts into pieces (30–40 cm long). Soak in water with 20 g Carbendazim (~₹300/pack) for 1 hour to prevent diseases.

  • Dig trenches 30 cm deep, 90 cm apart in the field.

  • What to Buy:

  • Sugarcane setts: ~₹4000–6000.

  • Carbendazim: ~₹300/pack.

  • Tip: Keep setts moist but not soggy before planting.

Growing Sugarcane (Weeks 1 to 48)

Week 1: Plant Setts

  • What to Do:
  • Place setts in trenches, 2 buds facing up, 30 cm apart. Cover with soil. Water well (like a good rain).

  • Keep field wet but not flooded (~5 cm water).

  • What to Buy: Nothing new.

  • Tip: Plant in the morning when it’s cool.

Weeks 2–8: Help Plants Grow Roots

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 7–10 days (~10 cm water).
  • Week 4: Spread 0.5 bag urea (25 kg, ~₹265) to help plants grow.

  • Week 6: Pull weeds by hand (~2 workers, ~₹1000) or use 1 L Pendimethalin in 200 L water (~₹500).

  • What to Buy:

  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.

  • Pendimethalin: ~₹500/L.

  • Tip: Check for weeds early to save plants.

Weeks 9–16: Help Plants Grow Stems

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 7–10 days (~10–15 cm water).
  • Week 10: Spread 0.5 bag urea (25 kg, ~₹265).

  • Week 12: Check for borers (holes in stems). Spray 500 ml Malathion in 200 L water (~₹400) if seen.

  • What to Buy:

  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.

  • Malathion: ~₹400/L.

  • Tip: Keep soil moist for strong stems.

Weeks 17–32: Support Growth

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 7–10 days (~15 cm water).
  • Week 20: Spread 0.5 bag urea (25 kg, ~₹265).

  • Week 24: Check for leaf spots (disease). Spray 500 g Mancozeb in 200 L water (~₹300) if seen.

  • What to Buy:

  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.

  • Mancozeb: ~₹300/kg.

  • Tip: Check plants weekly for bugs or spots.

Weeks 33–48: Prepare for Harvest

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 7–10 days, reduce to 5 cm in last 4 weeks.
  • Week 40: Check for borers again. Spray 500 ml Malathion (~₹400) if seen.

  • Week 48: Stop water 2 weeks before cutting. Cut canes when they’re thick and hard (~₹5000 for labor).

  • What to Buy:

  • Malathion: ~₹400/L.

  • Tip: Expect ~30–40 tons (~₹90,000–1,20,000 at ₹3000/ton).

After Collecting Sugarcane

  • What to Do:
  • Clear field of leftover canes. Store canes in a dry place.

  • Ask a farm helper about growing a ratoon crop (new plants from old roots) by watering again.

  • Tip: Rest the field or plan another crop to keep soil healthy.

Money You Spend and Earn

  • What You Spend:
  • Setts: ~₹4000–6000.
  • Plant food (fertilizers): ~₹3000–5000.
  • Pest sprays: ~₹1000–2000.
  • Workers (planting, weeding, cutting): ~₹4000–7000.

  • Total: ~₹12,000–20,000.

  • What You Earn: ~30–40 tons at ₹3000/ton = ~₹90,000–1,20,000. After costs, you keep ~₹70,000–1,08,000.

  • Tip: Save money by using cow dung instead of some fertilizers.

Easy Tips to Do Well

  • Water: Keep field wet, especially in hot months. Use a canal or pump if rain stops.
  • Check Plants: Walk field weekly to spot bugs or yellow leaves.
  • Be Safe: Wear a mask and gloves when spraying. Ask a farm helper about safe use.
  • Ask for Help: Talk to local farmers or a farm office for planting times or spray advice.
  • Best Time: Plant in February–March (check local timing).
