Papaya

Easy Guide to Grow Papaya on 1 Acre

This guide tells you how to grow papayas on 1 acre. It’s for anyone, even if you don’t read well or never grew papayas before. Your land is ready for papayas (loamy soil, high water ~1000–1500 mm, enough nutrients). You need 800–1000 papaya plants (use hybrid, first harvest in ~1 year, gives ~10–15 tons/year). I’ll tell you what to do each week, what to buy, and how to keep it simple.

Before Planting (Weeks -2 to -1)

Week -2: Fix the Field

  • What to Do:
  • Check soil test to confirm it’s good for papayas (pH 6–7). If too sour, add 1 cartload lime (~₹2000). If hard, add 1 cartload gypsum (~₹2000).
  • Plow field 2 times to make soil loose. Spread 1 cartload cow dung (~₹1000).

  • Add plant food: 0.5 bag urea (25 kg, ~₹265), 0.5 bag SSP (25 kg, ~₹200), 0.5 bag MOP (20 kg, ~₹1700).

  • What to Buy:

  • Lime/gypsum: ~₹2000/cartload.
  • Cow dung: ~₹1000/cartload.
  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.
  • SSP: ~₹200/bag.

  • MOP: ~₹1700/bag.

  • Tip: Loose soil helps papaya roots grow strong.

Week -1: Get Plants Ready

  • What to Do:
  • Buy 800–1000 hybrid papaya plants (~₹8000–10,000) from a trusted nursery.

  • Dig pits 45 cm deep, 45 cm wide, 2 m apart in rows. Mix soil with cow dung.

  • What to Buy:

  • Papaya plants: ~₹8000–10,000.

  • Tip: Choose healthy plants with green leaves.

Growing Papayas (Weeks 1 to 52)

Week 1: Plant Papayas

  • What to Do:
  • Plant papayas in pits. Cover roots with soil. Water well (like a good rain).

  • Keep soil moist but not flooded.

  • What to Buy: Nothing new.

  • Tip: Plant in the morning when it’s cool.

Weeks 2–12: Help Plants Grow Roots

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 5–7 days (~5 cm water).
  • Week 6: Spread 0.5 bag urea (25 kg, ~₹265).

  • Week 8: Pull weeds by hand (~2 workers, ~₹1000).

  • What to Buy:

  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.

  • Tip: Keep soil weed-free for strong roots.

Weeks 13–24: Help Plants Grow Stems

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 5–7 days. Week 16: Spread 0.5 bag urea (25 kg, ~₹265).

  • Week 20: Check for aphids. Spray 100 ml Imidacloprid in 200 L water (~₹400) if seen.

  • What to Buy:

  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.

  • Imidacloprid: ~₹400/bottle.

  • Tip: Check for bugs weekly.

Weeks 25–52: Pick Papayas

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 5–7 days. Week 32: Spread 0.5 bag MOP (20 kg, ~₹1700).
  • Week 40: Check for ring spot virus (yellow leaves). Spray 500 g Mancozeb in 200 L water (~₹300) if seen.

  • Pick papayas when yellowing (~₹5000 for labor). Store in a cool place (~₹1000 for transport).

  • What to Buy:

  • MOP: ~₹1700/bag.

  • Mancozeb: ~₹300/kg.

  • Tip: Expect ~10–15 tons/year (~₹1,00,000–1,50,000 at ₹10,000/ton).

After Collecting Papayas

  • What to Do:
  • Cut old plants after 2–3 years. Plant new ones.

  • Store papayas in a cool, dry place.

  • Tip: Check stored papayas for rot weekly.

Money You Spend and Earn

  • What You Spend:
  • Plants: ~₹8000–10,000.
  • Plant food (fertilizers): ~₹3000–5000.
  • Pest sprays: ~₹500–1000.
  • Workers (planting, weeding, picking): ~₹5000–8000.

  • Total: ~₹16,500–24,000.

  • What You Earn: ~10–15 tons/year at ₹10,000/ton = ~₹1,00,000–1,50,000/year. After costs, you keep ~₹76,000–1,33,500.

  • Tip: Save money by using cow dung.

Easy Tips to Do Well

  • Water: Keep soil moist, water every 5–7 days.
  • Check Plants: Walk field weekly to spot bugs or yellow leaves.
  • Be Safe: Wear a mask and gloves when spraying. Ask a farm helper about safe use.
  • Ask for Help: Talk to local farmers or a farm office for planting times or spray advice.
  • Best Time: Plant in June–July (check local timing).
