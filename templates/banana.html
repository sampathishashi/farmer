Banana

Easy Guide to Grow Banana on 1 Acre

This guide tells you how to grow banana on 1 acre. It’s for anyone, even if you don’t read well or never grew banana before. Your land is ready for banana (loamy soil, high water ~1500–2000 mm, enough nutrients). You need 800–1000 banana suckers (use Grand Naine, grows in ~40 weeks, gives ~10–15 tons). I’ll tell you what to do each week, what to buy, and how to keep it simple.

Before Planting (Weeks -2 to -1)

Week -2: Fix the Field

  • What to Do:
  • Check soil test to confirm it’s good for banana. If soil is too sour, add 1 cartload lime (~₹2000). If hard, add 1 cartload gypsum (~₹2000).
  • Plow field 2 times to make soil loose. Spread 2 cartloads cow dung (~₹1000/cartload).

  • Add plant food: 1 bag urea (50 kg, ~₹265), 1 bag SSP (50 kg, ~₹200), 0.5 bag MOP (20 kg, ~₹1700).

  • What to Buy:

  • Lime/gypsum: ~₹2000/cartload.
  • Cow dung: ~₹1000/cartload.
  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.
  • SSP: ~₹200/bag.

  • MOP: ~₹1700/bag.

  • Tip: Loose soil helps banana roots grow strong.

Week -1: Get Suckers Ready

  • What to Do:
  • Buy 800–1000 Grand Naine suckers (~₹8000–10,000) from a trusted nursery.

  • Dig pits 60 cm deep, 60 cm wide, 2 m apart in rows. Mix soil with cow dung.

  • What to Buy:

  • Banana suckers: ~₹8000–10,000.

  • Tip: Choose healthy suckers with strong roots.

Growing Banana (Weeks 1 to 40)

Week 1: Plant Suckers

  • What to Do:
  • Plant suckers in pits. Cover roots with soil. Water well (like a good rain).

  • Keep soil moist but not flooded.

  • What to Buy: Nothing new.

  • Tip: Plant in the morning when it’s cool.

Weeks 2–8: Help Plants Grow Roots

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 5–7 days (~10 cm water).
  • Week 4: Spread 0.5 bag urea (25 kg, ~₹265).

  • Week 6: Pull weeds by hand (~2 workers, ~₹1000).

  • What to Buy:

  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.

  • Tip: Keep soil moist and weed-free.

Weeks 9–16: Help Plants Grow Stems

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 5–7 days. Week 12: Spread 0.5 bag urea (25 kg, ~₹265).

  • Week 14: Check for leaf-eating bugs. Spray 500 ml Malathion in 200 L water (~₹400) if seen.

  • What to Buy:

  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.

  • Malathion: ~₹400/L.

  • Tip: Check leaves weekly for bugs.

Weeks 17–32: Support Fruit Growth

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 5–7 days (~15 cm water).
  • Week 20: Spread 0.5 bag MOP (20 kg, ~₹1700) for fruit growth.

  • Week 24: Check for fruit borers. Spray 500 ml Malathion (~₹400) if seen.

  • What to Buy:

  • MOP: ~₹1700/bag.

  • Malathion: ~₹400/L.

  • Tip: Support heavy fruit bunches with sticks.

Weeks 33–40: Pick Bananas

  • What to Do:
  • Water until 1 week before picking. Cut bunches when fruits are green but full (~₹5000 for labor).

  • Ripen fruits in a cool, dry place (~₹1000 for transport).

  • What to Buy: Nothing new.

  • Tip: Expect ~10–15 tons (~₹1,00,000–1,50,000 at ₹10,000/ton).

After Collecting Banana

  • What to Do:
  • Cut old plants. Keep 1–2 suckers per pit for the next crop.

  • Store bananas in a cool place to ripen.

  • Tip: Plan for a ratoon crop from suckers.

Money You Spend and Earn

  • What You Spend:
  • Suckers: ~₹8000–10,000.
  • Plant food (fertilizers): ~₹3000–5000.
  • Pest sprays: ~₹500–1000.
  • Workers (planting, weeding, picking): ~₹4000–7000.

  • Total: ~₹15,500–23,000.

  • What You Earn: ~10–15 tons at ₹10,000/ton = ~₹1,00,000–1,50,000. After costs, you keep ~₹77,000–1,34,500.

  • Tip: Save money by using cow dung.

Easy Tips to Do Well

  • Water: Keep soil wet, especially in dry months. Use a canal or pump.
  • Check Plants: Walk field weekly to spot bugs or yellow leaves.
  • Be Safe: Wear a mask and gloves when spraying. Ask a farm helper about safe use.
  • Ask for Help: Talk to local farmers or a farm office for planting times or spray advice.
  • Best Time: Plant in June–July (check local timing).
