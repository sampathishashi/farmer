Cotton

Easy Guide to Grow Cotton on 1 Acre

This guide tells you how to grow cotton on 1 acre. It’s for anyone, even if you don’t read well or never grew cotton before. Your land is ready for cotton (loamy or black soil, medium water ~500–900 mm, enough nutrients). You need 2–3 kg of cotton seeds (use Bt hybrid, grows in ~24 weeks, gives ~8–10 quintals).

Before Planting (Weeks -1 to 0)

Week -1: Fix the Field

  • What to Do:
  • Check soil test to confirm it’s good for cotton. If soil is too sour, add 1 cartload lime (~₹2000). If hard, add 1 cartload gypsum (~₹2000).
  • Plow field 2–3 times to make soil loose. Spread 1 cartload cow dung (~₹1000).

  • Add plant food: 1 bag urea (50 kg, ~₹265), 1 bag SSP (50 kg, ~₹200), 0.5 bag MOP (20 kg, ~₹1700).

  • What to Buy:

  • Lime/gypsum: ~₹2000/cartload.
  • Cow dung: ~₹1000/cartload.
  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.
  • SSP: ~₹200/bag.

  • MOP: ~₹1700/bag.

  • Tip: Loose soil helps cotton roots grow strong.

Growing Cotton (Weeks 1 to 24)

Week 1: Plant Seeds

  • What to Do:

  • Buy 2–3 kg Bt hybrid cotton seeds (~₹1000–2000). Sow seeds in rows 90 cm apart, 60 cm between plants. Water well after planting.

  • What to Buy:

  • Seeds: ~₹1000–2000.

  • Tip: Plant in the evening when it’s cool.

Weeks 2–6: Help Plants Grow Roots

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 7–10 days (like a good rain).
  • Week 4: Pull weeds by hand (~2 workers, ~₹1000) or use 0.5 L Pendimethalin (~₹500).

  • Week 5: Check for sucking pests (like aphids). Spray 100 ml Imidacloprid in 200 L water (~₹400) if seen.

  • What to Buy:

  • Pendimethalin: ~₹500/L.

  • Imidacloprid: ~₹400/bottle.

  • Tip: Check for weeds and bugs early.

Weeks 7–12: Help Plants Grow Stems and Flowers

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 7–10 days. Week 8: Spread 0.5 bag urea (25 kg, ~₹265) to help plants grow.

  • Week 10: Check for bollworms (holes in bolls). Spray 500 ml Malathion in 200 L water (~₹400) if seen.

  • What to Buy:

  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.

  • Malathion: ~₹400/L.

  • Tip: Keep soil moist during flowering.

Weeks 13–18: Support Boll Growth

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 7–10 days. Check bolls as they form.

  • Week 14: Spray 500 ml Malathion (~₹400) if bollworms return.

  • What to Buy:

  • Malathion: ~₹400/L.

  • Tip: Bolls should feel full and firm.

Weeks 19–24: Pick Cotton

  • What to Do:
  • Stop water 1 week before picking. Pick when bolls open (~₹5000 for labor, as it takes more time).

  • Dry cotton in the sun (~₹1000 for transport to market).

  • What to Buy: Nothing new.

  • Tip: Expect ~8–10 quintals (~₹32,000–40,000 at ₹4000/quintal).

After Collecting Cotton

  • What to Do:
  • Clear field of leftover plants. Store cotton in a dry place safe from bugs.

  • Plan another crop to keep soil healthy.

  • Tip: Check stored cotton for bugs monthly.

Money You Spend and Earn

  • What You Spend:
  • Seeds: ~₹1000–2000.
  • Plant food (fertilizers): ~₹2000–4000.
  • Pest sprays: ~₹500–1000.
  • Workers (planting, weeding, picking): ~₹4000–7000.

  • Total: ~₹7500–14,000.

  • What You Earn: ~8–10 quintals at ₹4000/quintal = ~₹32,000–40,000. After costs, you keep ~₹18,000–32,500.

  • Tip: Save money by using cow dung instead of some fertilizers.

Easy Tips to Do Well

  • Water: Cotton needs medium water. Water every 7–10 days.
  • Check Plants: Walk field weekly to spot bugs or yellow leaves.
  • Be Safe: Wear a mask and gloves when spraying. Ask a farm helper about safe use.
  • Ask for Help: Talk to local farmers or a farm office for planting times or spray advice.
  • Best Time: Plant in May–June (check local timing).
