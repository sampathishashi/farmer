Sorghum

Easy Guide to Grow Sorghum (Jowar) on 1 Acre

This guide tells you how to grow sorghum on 1 acre. It’s for anyone, even beginners. Your land is ready for sorghum (sandy or muddy soil, low water ~400–600 mm, some nutrients). You need 4–5 kg of sorghum seeds (use hybrid, grows in ~16 weeks, gives ~15–18 quintals).

Before Planting (Weeks -1 to 0)

Week -1: Fix the Field

  • What to Do:
  • Check soil test. If sour, add 1 cartload lime (~₹2000). If hard, add 1 cartload gypsum (~₹2000).
  • Plow field 2 times to loosen soil. Spread 0.5 cartload cow dung (~₹500).

  • Add plant food: 0.5 bag urea (25 kg, ~₹265), 0.5 bag SSP (25 kg, ~₹200).

  • What to Buy:

  • Lime/gypsum: ~₹2000/cartload.
  • Cow dung: ~₹500/half cartload.
  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.

  • SSP: ~₹200/bag.

  • Tip: Loose soil helps sorghum grow well.

Growing Sorghum (Weeks 1 to 16)

Week 1: Plant Seeds

  • What to Do:

  • Buy 4–5 kg hybrid seeds (~₹500–1000). Sow seeds in rows 45 cm apart. Water lightly.

  • What to Buy:

  • Seeds: ~₹500–1000.

  • Tip: Plant when soil is warm.

Weeks 2–4: Help Plants Grow Roots

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 10–12 days (like a light rain).

  • Week 3: Pull weeds (~2 workers, ~₹1000).

  • What to Buy: Nothing new.

  • Tip: Remove weeds early to help plants.

Weeks 5–8: Help Plants Grow Stems

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 10–12 days. Week 6: Spread 0.5 bag urea (25 kg, ~₹265).

  • Week 7: Check for shoot flies (small bugs on leaves). Spray 500 ml Malathion in 200 L water (~₹400) if seen.

  • What to Buy:

  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.

  • Malathion: ~₹400/L.

  • Tip: Keep soil moist but not too wet.

Weeks 9–12: Support Grain Growth

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 10–12 days. Check grains as they form.

  • Week 10: Spray 500 ml Malathion (~₹400) if bugs return.

  • What to Buy:

  • Malathion: ~₹400/L.

  • Tip: Grains should feel hard.

Weeks 13–16: Cut and Collect Sorghum

  • What to Do:
  • Stop water 1 week before cutting. Cut when grains are hard (~₹2800 for labor).

  • Dry grains in sun (~₹1000 for transport).

  • What to Buy: Nothing new.

  • Tip: Expect ~15–18 quintals (~₹30,000–36,000 at ₹2000/quintal).

After Collecting Sorghum

  • What to Do:
  • Clear field of stalks. Store grains in a dry place.

  • Plan another crop to keep soil healthy.

  • Tip: Check stored grains for bugs.

Money You Spend and Earn

  • What You Spend:
  • Seeds: ~₹500–1000.
  • Plant food: ~₹1000–2000.
  • Pest sprays: ~₹500–1000.
  • Workers: ~₹3000–5000.

  • Total: ~₹5000–10,000.

  • What You Earn: ~15–18 quintals at ₹2000/quintal = ~₹30,000–36,000. After costs, you keep ~₹20,000–31,000.

  • Tip: Use cow dung to save on fertilizers.

Easy Tips to Do Well

  • Water: Needs less water than maize. Water every 10–12 days.
  • Check Plants: Look for bugs weekly.
  • Be Safe: Wear mask when spraying.
  • Ask for Help: Ask local farmers for planting times.
  • Best Time: Plant in June–July (check local timing).
