Groundnut

Easy Guide to Grow Groundnut on 1 Acre

This guide tells you how to grow groundnut on 1 acre. It’s for anyone, even beginners. Your land is ready for groundnut (sandy or loamy soil, medium water ~500–700 mm, some nutrients). You need 40–50 kg of seeds (use hybrid, grows in ~16 weeks, gives ~10–12 quintals).

Before Planting (Weeks -1 to 0)

Week -1: Fix the Field

  • What to Do:
  • Check soil test. If sour, add 0.5 cartload lime (~₹1000). If hard, add 0.5 cartload gypsum (~₹1000).
  • Plow field 2 times to loosen soil. Spread 1 cartload cow dung (~₹1000).

  • Add plant food: 0.5 bag SSP (25 kg, ~₹200), 0.5 bag MOP (20 kg, ~₹1700).

  • What to Buy:

  • Lime/gypsum: ~₹1000/half cartload.
  • Cow dung: ~₹1000/cartload.
  • SSP: ~₹200/bag.

  • MOP: ~₹1700/bag.

  • Tip: Loose soil helps groundnut pods form.

Growing Groundnut (Weeks 1 to 16)

Week 1: Plant Seeds

  • What to Do:

  • Buy 40–50 kg hybrid seeds (~₹2000–3000). Sow seeds in rows 45 cm apart. Water well.

  • What to Buy:

  • Seeds: ~₹2000–3000.

  • Tip: Plant when soil is warm.

Weeks 2–4: Help Plants Grow Roots

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 7–10 days (like a good rain).

  • Week 3: Pull weeds (~2 workers, ~₹1000).

  • What to Buy: Nothing new.

  • Tip: Remove weeds early.

Weeks 5–8: Help Plants Grow Stems

  • What to Do:

  • Water every 7–10 days. Week 6: Check for leaf-eating bugs. Spray 500 ml Malathion in 200 L water (~₹400) if seen.

  • What to Buy:

  • Malathion: ~₹400/L.

  • Tip: Keep soil moist during pod formation.

Weeks 9–12: Support Pod Growth

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 7–10 days. Check pods as they form.

  • Week 10: Spray 500 ml Malathion (~₹400) if bugs return.

  • What to Buy:

  • Malathion: ~₹400/L.

  • Tip: Pods should feel full underground.

Weeks 13–16: Dig Groundnut

  • What to Do:
  • Stop water 1 week before digging. Dig when pods are hard (~₹2800 for labor).

  • Dry pods in sun (~₹1000 for transport).

  • What to Buy: Nothing new.

  • Tip: Expect ~10–12 quintals (~₹30,000–36,000 at ₹3000/quintal).

After Collecting Groundnut

  • What to Do:
  • Clear field of plants. Store pods in a dry place.

  • Plan another crop.

  • Tip: Check stored pods for bugs.

Money You Spend and Earn

  • What You Spend:
  • Seeds: ~₹2000–3000.
  • Plant food: ~₹2000–4000.
  • Pest sprays: ~₹500–1000.
  • Workers: ~₹3000–5000.

  • Total: ~₹7500–13,000.

  • What You Earn: ~10–12 quintals at ₹3000/quintal = ~₹30,000–36,000. After costs, you keep ~₹17,000–28,500.

  • Tip: Use cow dung to save money.

Easy Tips to Do Well

  • Water: Water every 7–10 days, not too much.
  • Check Plants: Look for bugs in pods weekly.
  • Be Safe: Wear mask when spraying.
  • Ask for Help: Ask local farmers for planting times.
  • Best Time: Plant in June–July (check local timing).
