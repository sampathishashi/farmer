Coffee

Easy Guide to Grow Coffee on 1 Acre

This guide tells you how to grow coffee on 1 acre. It’s for anyone, even if you don’t read well or never grew coffee before. Your land is ready for coffee (sandy loamy soil, high water ~1500–2000 mm, acidic soil). You need 1000–1500 coffee plants (use Robusta, first harvest in ~52 weeks, gives ~2–3 quintals/year). I’ll tell you what to do each week, what to buy, and how to keep it simple.

Before Planting (Weeks -2 to -1)

Week -2: Fix the Field

  • What to Do:
  • Check soil test to confirm it’s acidic (pH 5–6). If not, add 1 cartload lime (~₹2000) to adjust.
  • Plow field 2 times to make soil loose. Spread 1 cartload cow dung (~₹1000).

  • Add plant food: 0.5 bag urea (25 kg, ~₹265), 0.5 bag SSP (25 kg, ~₹200).

  • What to Buy:

  • Lime: ~₹2000/cartload.
  • Cow dung: ~₹1000/cartload.
  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.

  • SSP: ~₹200/bag.

  • Tip: Acidic soil is best for coffee. Ask a farm helper to check pH.

Week -1: Get Plants Ready

  • What to Do:
  • Buy 1000–1500 Robusta coffee plants (~₹6000–8000) from a trusted nursery.

  • Dig pits 45 cm deep, 45 cm wide, 60 cm apart in rows. Mix soil with cow dung.

  • What to Buy:

  • Coffee plants: ~₹6000–8000.

  • Tip: Choose healthy plants with green leaves.

Growing Coffee (Weeks 1 to 52)

Week 1: Plant Coffee

  • What to Do:
  • Plant coffee plants in pits. Cover roots with soil. Water well (like a good rain).

  • Keep soil moist but not flooded.

  • What to Buy: Nothing new.

  • Tip: Plant in the morning when it’s cool.

Weeks 2–12: Help Plants Grow Roots

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 5–7 days (~5 cm water).
  • Week 4: Spread 0.5 bag urea (25 kg, ~₹265).

  • Week 6: Pull weeds by hand (~2 workers, ~₹1000).

  • What to Buy:

  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.

  • Tip: Keep soil moist and weed-free.

Weeks 13–24: Help Plants Grow Stems

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 5–7 days. Week 16: Spread 0.5 bag urea (25 kg, ~₹265).

  • Week 20: Check for berry borers (holes in berries). Spray 500 ml Malathion in 200 L water (~₹400) if seen.

  • What to Buy:

  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.

  • Malathion: ~₹400/L.

  • Tip: Check berries weekly for bugs.

Weeks 25–52: Prepare for First Harvest

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 5–7 days. Week 32: Spread 0.5 bag urea (25 kg, ~₹265).
  • Week 40: Check for leaf rust (orange spots). Spray 500 g Mancozeb in 200 L water (~₹300) if seen.

  • Week 52: Pick red berries by hand (~₹3000 for labor).

  • What to Buy:

  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.

  • Mancozeb: ~₹300/kg.

  • Tip: Expect ~2–3 quintals/year (~₹20,000–30,000 at ₹10,000/quintal).

After Collecting Coffee

  • What to Do:
  • Dry berries in the sun for 7–10 days. Store in a dry place.

  • Prune plants lightly to keep them healthy.

  • Tip: Check stored berries for bugs or mold.

Money You Spend and Earn

  • What You Spend:
  • Plants: ~₹6000–8000.
  • Plant food (fertilizers): ~₹2000–4000.
  • Pest sprays: ~₹500–1000.
  • Workers (planting, weeding, picking): ~₹3000–6000.

  • Total: ~₹11,500–19,000.

  • What You Earn: ~2–3 quintals/year at ₹10,000/quintal = ~₹20,000–30,000/year. After costs, you keep ~₹1000–18,500 (first year).

  • Tip: Save money by using cow dung. Earnings increase in later years.

Easy Tips to Do Well

  • Water: Keep soil moist, especially in dry months. Use a canal or pump.
  • Check Plants: Walk field weekly to spot bugs or yellow leaves.
  • Be Safe: Wear a mask and gloves when spraying. Ask a farm helper about safe use.
  • Ask for Help: Talk to local farmers or a farm office for planting times or spray advice.
  • Best Time: Plant in June–July (check local timing).
