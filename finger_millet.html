Finger Millet

Easy Guide to Grow Finger Millet (Ragi) on 1 Acre

This guide tells you how to grow ragi on 1 acre. It’s for anyone, even beginners. Your land is ready for ragi (loamy or sandy soil, low water ~350–550 mm, some nutrients). You need 2–3 kg of ragi seeds (use hybrid, grows in ~16 weeks, gives ~10–12 quintals).

Before Planting (Weeks -1 to 0)

Week -1: Fix the Field

  • What to Do:
  • Check soil test. If sour, add 0.5 cartload lime (~₹1000). If hard, add 0.5 cartload gypsum (~₹1000).
  • Plow field 2 times to loosen soil. Spread 0.5 cartload cow dung (~₹500).

  • Add plant food: 0.5 bag urea (25 kg, ~₹265).

  • What to Buy:

  • Lime/gypsum: ~₹1000/half cartload.
  • Cow dung: ~₹500/half cartload.

  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.

  • Tip: Loose soil helps ragi grow strong.

Growing Finger Millet (Weeks 1 to 16)

Week 1: Plant Seeds

  • What to Do:

  • Buy 2–3 kg hybrid seeds (~₹300–600). Sow seeds in rows 30 cm apart. Water lightly.

  • What to Buy:

  • Seeds: ~₹300–600.

  • Tip: Plant when soil is warm.

Weeks 2–4: Help Plants Grow Roots

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 10 days (lightly).

  • Week 3: Pull weeds (~2 workers, ~₹1000).

  • What to Buy: Nothing new.

  • Tip: Remove weeds early.

Weeks 5–8: Help Plants Grow Stems

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 10 days. Week 6: Spread 0.5 bag urea (25 kg, ~₹265).

  • Week 7: Check for blast disease (brown spots on leaves). Spray 500 g Mancozeb in 200 L water (~₹300) if seen.

  • What to Buy:

  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.

  • Mancozeb: ~₹300/kg.

  • Tip: Keep soil slightly moist.

Weeks 9–12: Support Grain Growth

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 10 days. Check grains as they form.

  • Week 10: Spray 500 g Mancozeb (~₹300) if disease returns.

  • What to Buy:

  • Mancozeb: ~₹300/kg.

  • Tip: Grains should feel hard.

Weeks 13–16: Cut and Collect Ragi

  • What to Do:
  • Stop water 1 week before cutting. Cut when grains are hard (~₹2800 for labor).

  • Dry grains in sun (~₹1000 for transport).

  • What to Buy: Nothing new.

  • Tip: Expect ~10–12 quintals (~₹20,000–24,000 at ₹2000/quintal).

After Collecting Finger Millet

  • What to Do:
  • Clear field of stalks. Store grains in a dry place.

  • Plan another crop.

  • Tip: Check stored grains for bugs.

Money You Spend and Earn

  • What You Spend:
  • Seeds: ~₹300–600.
  • Plant food: ~₹1000–2000.
  • Pest sprays: ~₹500–1000.
  • Workers: ~₹3000–5000.

  • Total: ~₹4800–8600.

  • What You Earn: ~10–12 quintals at ₹2000/quintal = ~₹20,000–24,000. After costs, you keep ~₹11,400–19,200.

  • Tip: Use cow dung to save money.

Easy Tips to Do Well

  • Water: Needs little water. Water every 10 days.
  • Check Plants: Look for diseases weekly.
  • Be Safe: Wear mask when spraying.
  • Ask for Help: Ask local farmers for planting times.
  • Best Time: Plant in June–July (check local timing).
