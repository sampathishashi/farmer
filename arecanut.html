Arecanut

Easy Guide to Grow Arecanut on 1 Acre

This guide tells you how to grow arecanut on 1 acre. It’s for anyone, even if you don’t read well or never grew arecanut before. Your land is ready for arecanut (loamy soil, high water ~1000–1500 mm, enough nutrients). You need 100–150 arecanut trees (use hybrid, first harvest in ~3–5 years, gives ~2–3 tons/year). I’ll tell you what to do each week, what to buy, and how to keep it simple.

Before Planting (Weeks -2 to -1)

Week -2: Fix the Field

  • What to Do:
  • Check soil test to confirm it’s good for arecanut (pH 5.5–7). If too sour, add 1 cartload lime (~₹2000). If hard, add 1 cartload gypsum (~₹2000).
  • Plow field 2 times to make soil loose. Spread 1 cartload cow dung (~₹1000).

  • Add plant food: 0.5 bag urea (25 kg, ~₹265), 0.5 bag SSP (25 kg, ~₹200), 0.5 bag MOP (20 kg, ~₹1700).

  • What to Buy:

  • Lime/gypsum: ~₹2000/cartload.
  • Cow dung: ~₹1000/cartload.
  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.
  • SSP: ~₹200/bag.

  • MOP: ~₹1700/bag.

  • Tip: Loose soil helps arecanut roots grow strong.

Week -1: Get Trees Ready

  • What to Do:
  • Buy 100–150 hybrid arecanut seedlings (~₹5000–7500) from a trusted nursery.

  • Dig pits 60 cm deep, 60 cm wide, 5 m apart in rows. Mix soil with cow dung.

  • What to Buy:

  • Arecanut seedlings: ~₹5000–7500.

  • Tip: Choose healthy seedlings with strong roots.

Growing Arecanut (Weeks 1 to Year 5)

Week 1: Plant Trees

  • What to Do:
  • Plant seedlings in pits. Cover roots with soil. Water well (like a good rain).

  • Keep soil moist but not flooded.

  • What to Buy: Nothing new.

  • Tip: Plant in the morning when it’s cool.

Weeks 2–12: Help Trees Grow Roots

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 7–10 days (~5 cm water).
  • Week 6: Spread 0.5 bag urea (25 kg, ~₹265).

  • Week 8: Pull weeds by hand (~2 workers, ~₹1000).

  • What to Buy:

  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.

  • Tip: Keep soil weed-free for strong roots.

Weeks 13–52 (Year 1): Help Trees Grow

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 7–10 days. Week 24: Spread 0.5 bag urea (25 kg, ~₹265).
  • Week 32: Check for bud rot (yellow leaves). Spray 500 g Mancozeb in 200 L water (~₹300) if seen.

  • Prune dry leaves in winter (~₹1000 for labor).

  • What to Buy:

  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.

  • Mancozeb: ~₹300/kg.

  • Tip: Check for bugs weekly.

Years 2–4: Support Growth

  • What to Do:
  • Water every 7–10 days. Spread 0.5 bag urea and 0.5 bag MOP yearly (~₹1965).
  • Check for nut weevils. Spray 500 ml Malathion in 200 L water (~₹400) if seen.

  • Prune dry leaves yearly (~₹1000/year).

  • What to Buy:

  • Urea: ~₹265/bag.
  • MOP: ~₹1700/bag.

  • Malathion: ~₹400/L.

  • Tip: Pruning keeps trees healthy.

Year 5: Pick Arecanuts

  • What to Do:
  • Water until 1 month before picking. Pick mature nuts (green or orange) (~₹5000 for labor).

  • Dry nuts in the sun (~₹1000 for transport).

  • What to Buy: Nothing new.

  • Tip: Expect ~2–3 tons/year (~₹60,000–90,000 at ₹30,000/ton).

After Collecting Arecanuts

  • What to Do:
  • Prune trees after harvest. Rest the soil or add cow dung.

  • Store nuts in a dry place.

  • Tip: Check stored nuts for rot monthly.

Money You Spend and Earn

  • What You Spend:
  • Seedlings: ~₹5000–7500.
  • Plant food (fertilizers): ~₹3000–6000 (over 5 years).
  • Pest sprays: ~₹500–1000.
  • Workers (planting, weeding, pruning, picking): ~₹5000–8000.

  • Total: ~₹13,500–22,500 (over 5 years).

  • What You Earn: ~2–3 tons/year at ₹30,000/ton = ~₹60,000–90,000/year. After costs, you keep ~₹37,500–76,500 (first harvest year).

  • Tip: Save money by using cow dung.

Easy Tips to Do Well

  • Water: Keep soil moist, water every 7–10 days.
  • Check Trees: Walk field weekly to spot bugs or yellow leaves.
  • Be Safe: Wear a mask and gloves when spraying. Ask a farm helper about safe use.
  • Ask for Help: Talk to local farmers or a farm office for planting times or spray advice.
  • Best Time: Plant in June–July (check local timing).
