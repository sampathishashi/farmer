🌱 Fertilizer Recommendations

  • Nitrogen (N): Apply Urea (46-0-0) at 100-150 kg/ha to increase nitrogen levels
  • Phosphorus (P): Phosphorus is optimal; maintain current application rates
  • Potassium (K): Reduce Potassium fertilizer to avoid excess accumulation
  • pH Management: Consider adding agricultural lime to slightly raise soil pH
  • Organic Matter: Add farmyard manure or compost to improve soil structure
Important: Always follow local agricultural guidelines and consult with agricultural experts for proper fertilizer application rates and timing.

💰 Where to Sell Your Crops

🏪 Local Markets

Visit the nearest mandi or farmer's market for direct sales. Best for fresh produce and small quantities.

  • Direct customer interaction
  • No transportation costs
  • Immediate payment

🏢 Regional Buyers

Contact local grain merchants, cooperatives, or food processing companies for bulk sales.

  • Higher volume sales
  • Stable pricing
  • Long-term contracts possible

💻 Online Platforms

List your crops on AgriMarket or similar online agri-marketplaces for wider reach.

  • Access to larger markets
  • Price transparency
  • Digital payment options
Tip: Check current market prices and demand trends before selling for maximum returns. Consider crop storage options if prices are expected to rise.
